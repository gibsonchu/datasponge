OPEN SPECIFICATIONS · DRAFT 0.1

Three standards.
One shared language.

Practical structures for publishing flood resilience information—whether you work with Floodline or maintain your own dataset.

Shared by design

Portable IDs, geography, provenance, organizations, taxonomy, dates, attribution, licensing, verification, and explicit relationships. A photograph can connect to a recovery guide and a local assistance provider.

OPEN INFRASTRUCTURE, SHARED STEWARDSHIP

A commons for
flood resilience.

DataSponge organizes information so more people can use it, connect it, and build on it.

Floodline is the founding steward. Blue Dots is a community-facing product that can consume DataSponge data. Other organizations can adopt the same specifications and publish compatible records independently.

Explore freely. Contribute together.

Browsing published records does not require an account. To contribute to this catalog, contact Floodline to discuss your work and arrange access. Contributions are reviewed before publication.

Contact Floodline ↗

Open by intention

The specifications and reusable code are open source. Rights to photographs, videos, and other third-party material remain with their owners and are recorded separately.

Our early focus is New York City. The pilot is a starting sample—not an exhaustive directory, a live incident feed, or a guarantee that a service is available.
